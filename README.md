## 🚁 Nomad — Drone Engineering Division

Nomad designs and manufactures a full spectrum of unmanned aerial systems — from professional edge-AI mission platforms to record-chasing FPV speed machines. Every aircraft below ships with source CAD/STL files, assembly manuals, and frame-only variants.

### Max — *Genesis Edge AI Professional Drone*
A professional edge-AI quadcopter built around a T700 carbon-fiber tube airframe, glass-filled nylon enclosures, and onboard NVIDIA Jetson Orin compute (Nano up to 40 TOPS / NX up to 100 TOPS). It runs ARK Flow / PX4 avionics, carries up to 4 kg of modular dovetail-rail payload, flies up to 30 minutes on 15-inch carbon props, and targets defense R&D, autonomy research, mapping, and industrial inspection with NDAA-focused sourcing.

### Moose — *1600mm Fixed-Wing UAV*
A 1600mm-wingspan, twin-tractor-motor, V-tail fixed-wing platform with removable wings, a swappable modular nose, and a central payload bay. 3D-printed in lightweight LW-PLA/LW-ASA and reinforced with carbon-tube spars, it's built for long-range FPV, aerial mapping, and terrain survey (2–4.5 kg AUW, 60–70 km/h cruise, Selig S3021 airfoil).

### Blackbee — *High-Speed FPV Quadcopter*
An advanced 8S high-speed FPV quad pairing a streamlined printed shell with a carbon main frame, 2312/1700kV power, a Fettec 8S 65A ESC, and a servo-driven camera tilt mechanism (up to ~90°). Built for aggressive forward-flight performance and serious DIY pilots.

### Whitebee — *SUB400speed Record Drone*
A sub-400g FPV speed quadcopter on the Armattan Movr163 CP2 frame with aerodynamic printed fairings, a 6S T-Motor V2208/1950KV powertrain, and Walksnail HD FPV. Engineered for 300+ km/h speed runs — **313 km/h proven** in spec testing.

### Vixen — *4-Inch Dead Pushy Cat*
A custom 3D-printed 4-inch FPV drone using a dead-cat + pusher geometry to clear the props from the camera view. Built around a 20×20 stack, DJI O4 Pro camera/VTX, ELRS, GPS, and a 6S powertrain, printed in PA-CF/PET-CF with TPU mounts and skids.

### Wings — *Flying-Wing Platform*
A flying-wing UAV platform in the Nomad lineup, available alongside its dedicated frame kit.

> Each model is also offered as a bare frame: `max-frame`, `moose-frame`, `blackbee-frame`, `whitebee-frame`, `vixen-frame`, `wings-frame`.
